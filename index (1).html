FAQ

Good questions

Where does the data come from?

Klozer is built on the public FMCSA Company Census. We clean it, remove duplicates and add new carriers on a monthly refresh.

How current are the contact details?

Contact details come from public filings and may not always be up to date. We recommend verifying before outreach.

How do I get access?

Click “Request access” and create an account. We review each new user and approve access personally, usually within one business day.

Can my whole team use it?

Yes. The Growth plan includes team access, and Enterprise can be tailored to your team size.

Is Klozer affiliated with the FMCSA?

No. Klozer is an independent product that organizes publicly available data. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the FMCSA or the U.S. Department of Transportation.
