FAQ

Good questions

What is the Klozer Score?

A rating from 1 to 100 for every carrier, based on fleet size, how recently the carrier registered and how complete its contact details are. A higher score means a better prospect to call first. It is included on every plan.

How current are the contact details?

Contact details come from public filings and may not always be up to date. We recommend verifying before outreach.

How do I get access?

Subscribe to a plan or send us a request. We review each account personally, usually within one business day, and then email you a welcome message with your sign-in link. Klozer is invite-only, so there is no public sign-up.

How does my team sign in and what can they see?

As the account owner you add each salesperson from the Team page. They get their own pipeline and territory, can see what teammates are working on, and sign in with a one-time code sent to their email. Only owners see the Reports page.

How many people can use one plan?

Starter includes 1 seat, Growth up to 3 and Enterprise up to 10. Need more? Email us and we will tailor a plan.
